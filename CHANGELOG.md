# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - unreleased

### Added
- Every new End city comes with an End ship, so every city has an Elytra to find. Each city is still an ordinary vanilla city: when one comes out without a ship, its layout is rolled again until it has one.
- `shipChance` setting: the percent of new End cities that get a ship (100 by default; vanilla is about 1 in 5).
- Installed without Mycel, the game still starts: the mod stays off and tells joining players that Mycel is missing, with a download link.
