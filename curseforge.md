# Guaranteed End Ships

**Every End city has a ship.** No more flying from city to city looking for an Elytra: with this mod, every new End city comes with its End ship, its Elytra and its dragon head.

## How it works

- In vanilla, only about one End city in five has a ship
- With this mod, when a city would be generated without a ship, its layout is rolled again until it has one
- Every city is still a normal vanilla city with vanilla loot, so nothing looks out of place
- Great for servers: more ships means more Elytras to go around

## Good to know

- Server side only: players don't need to install anything, and vanilla players can join
- Only End cities generated after you install it get a ship; cities you already found stay as they are
- Setting: `shipChance` (100 by default) if you want ships to be common but not guaranteed
- Fabric, Forge and NeoForge, Minecraft 26.1 to 26.3
- Requires [Mycel](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mycel). Without it the game still starts, and the mod tells players in chat that Mycel is missing
